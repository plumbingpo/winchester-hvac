Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Norfolk

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
